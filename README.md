Participation and community engagement within the Lattice ecosystem.

Lattice Community Groups (LCG) provide structured pathways for practitioners, stakeholders, contributors, and community members to participate in the ongoing development of the Lattice ecosystem.

LCG exists to encourage participation without transferring architectural, governance, or research authority to the participation layer itself.

## Mission

The mission of Lattice Community Groups is to provide a participation surface through which individuals, organisations, and communities can contribute knowledge, experience, feedback, and perspective.

Participation helps inform the ecosystem while preserving the distinction between contribution, governance, architecture, and research.

## Areas of Participation

### Community Engagement

Supporting discussion and engagement across the ecosystem.

### Practitioner Contribution

Bringing practical experience and implementation perspectives into community processes.

### Standards Participation

Contributing feedback and commentary to standards and specification development processes.

### Community Working Groups

Supporting collaborative exploration of ecosystem-related topics and initiatives.

## Participation Without Authority

Participation is intentionally separated from governance, architecture, and research.

Community contribution can inform ecosystem development without becoming canonical authority.

This distinction helps preserve meaningful participation while maintaining clear responsibility boundaries.

## The Lattice Semantic Nexus

The Lattice Semantic Nexus is structured around four distinct but related functions:

- **Lattice Core** — Architecture
- **LSN Foundation** — Governance and Stewardship
- **Lattice Research Institute (LRI)** — Independent Research and Analysis
- **Lattice Community Groups (LCG)** — Participation and Community Engagement
